How it works

From request to payroll-ready in four steps.

Everyone follows the same simple path, and your leave policy is applied the same way every time.

  1. 1

    Employee asks

    They pick dates and see straight away how many working days it costs and what's left, with weekends and public holidays already skipped.

  2. 2

    Manager decides

    One click to approve or reject, with a clear warning if too many people on the team would be away at once.

  3. 3

    HR steps in when needed

    Long or unpaid leave comes to HR for a second look. Anything left waiting for 48 hours comes to HR too.

  4. 4

    Balances update

    The days come off the balance the moment it's approved, and go back if the leave is cancelled. Nothing to type in twice.

Questions

Good to know.

Does it know about public holidays and our weekend?

Yes. Fridays, Saturdays and Egyptian public holidays are never counted as leave days, so employees and HR always see the same number.

What happens if a manager doesn't respond?

After 48 hours the request moves to HR automatically. The employee can see it marked as escalated on their request page.

Can HR fix a balance that's wrong?

Yes. HR adds a correction with a reason. The original entries are kept, so you can always see how a balance was reached.

Is employee information kept private?

Each person only sees what their role allows. Managers and HR confirm their sign-in with a code from their phone, and everyone is signed out after 15 minutes of inactivity.
